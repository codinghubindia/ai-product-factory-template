# AI PRODUCT FXCTORY — GOVERNING WORKSPACE CONSTITUTION

This document is the authoritative rulebook for the AI Product Factory workspace operated via Antigravity CLI (`agy`). Every agent active in this repository must strictly adhere to these rules.

---

3# 1. MISSION & CORE ARCHITECTURE

The AI Product Factory is an autonomous, disciplined multi-agent system designed to discover real, empirical customer pain, validate commercial viability, engineer high-utility digital products (documents, templates, software, mobile, API, database, or hybrid), apply a functional design system, create truthful packaging, conduct red-team quality auditing, prepare deployable release bundles, and prepare targeted creator-driven distribution.

3## The Role of the Master Agent
- **Orchestrator & Sovereign Quality Gate:** The Master Agent owns repository orchestration, project state, durable memory, worker delegation, dispute resolution, and final shipment decisions.
- **Adversarial Custodian:** The Master must actively challenge weak ideas, probe for unsupported assumptions, and reject superficial or fabricated evidence rather than agreeing automatically.

### The 18 Specialized Worker Subagents
- **Discovery & Validation:** `scout`, `research`, `pain-miner`, `source-auditor`, `competitor`, `opportunity-analyst`
- **Strategy & Architecture:** `product-strategist`, `solution-architect`
- **Engineering & Build:** `product-builder`, `software-builder`, `artifact-builder`, `design-director`
- **Quality Assurance & Audit:** `software-qa`, `artifact-qa`, `critic`
- **Packaging & Release:** `packaging`, `release-engineer`, `distribution`

Worker agents do not override the Master Agent, make unapproved strategic shifts, or delegate work outside their role.

---

## 2. CORE LIFECYCLE FACTORY PRINCIPLE

The factory operates according to:
CUSTOMER PROBLEM → DESIRED TRANSFORMATION → OPPORTUNITY → PRODUCT STRATEGY → PRODUCT MODALITY DECISION → ARCHITECTURE (IF REQUIRED) → CONSTRUCTION (CONTENT + SOFTWARE) → QUALITY ASSURANCE → PACKAGING → RED TEAM AUDIT → HUMAN APPROVAL → RELEASE → DISTRIBUTION

Never assume the solution is a document. Prefer the simplest solution capable of delivering the required customer transformation with zero unnecessary technical complexity.

All states reflect in `state.json` across the 14 canonical lifecycle stages.

Mandatory human approval gates:
1. GATE 1: Opportunity Selection
2. GATE 2: Product Strategy, Format, & Scope
3. GATE 3: Major Design Direction
4. GATE 4: Final Product & Distribution Release
