# Creative Marketing & Creator Engine Skill

Creative marketing in the AI Product Factory is not about hype, clickbait, or artificial urgency. It is the discipline of making the product's **real utility, signature mechanism, and transformation** immediately obvious and compelling to prospective customers.

---

## 1. Ethical Foundations of Truthful Marketing

- **Direct Emergence:** Every marketing hook, case study, and demonstration must emerge directly from an inspected, functioning feature in the delivered product.
- **Zero Hype / Fake Claims:** Never fabricate statistics, fake before/after metrics, or non-existent celebrity endorsements.
- **Problem Agitation Without Deception:** Frame the customer's existing pain clearly by describing observable symptoms (e.g. "PR reviews lingering for 4 days") without resorting to catastrophic fearmongering.

---

## 2. The 60-Second Demonstration Engine

Every creator-promoted or digital product must pass the **60-Second Demonstration Test**:

> *Can an expert or creator demonstrate the core transformation live on screen in under 60 seconds without hand-waving?*

### The 4 Demonstration Archetypes
1. **The Live Calculation:** Creator enters 3 real messy numbers into the financial model $\rightarrow$ the scenario waterfall instantly updates and flags a $45,000 blind spot in red.
2. **The 3-Click Diagnostic:** Creator selects 4 team symptoms in the interactive assessment $\rightarrow$ the radar chart outputs their exact operational bottleneck score and customized 3-step remediation.
3. **The Before / After Split:** Creator shows a messy 20-column unformatted spreadsheet next to the clean, frozen-pane, formatted output running in the deliverable.
4. **The CLI / Software Velocity Test:** Creator runs a single terminal command $\rightarrow$ 48 automated contract tests execute in 1.4 seconds with zero errors.

If a product cannot be demonstrated in under 60 seconds, the product strategy must be refined to make its transformation visible faster.

## 3. The 12 Creative Marketing Formats

The `marketing-strategist` creates content assets across 12 high-converting, ethical formats in `products/<product_id>/marketing/`:

1. **The Architecture Teardown:** A technical breakdown of why common industry approaches fail (e.g. "Why standard agile standups actually increase PR review latency").
2. **The Contrarian Truth Hook:** Challenging accepted industry dogma with empirical data from `memory/sources.csv`.
3. **The 5-Day Micro-Challenge:** A structured daily prompt series derived directly from the implementation workbook.
4. **The Interactive Mini-Diagnostic:** A free 2-minute web calculator acting as an entry-point lead magnet.
5. **The "Behind the Blueprint" Walkthrough:** A high-craft tour showing the internal formulas, schemas, or design system details.
6. **The Incident Case Study:** A step-by-step post-mortem showing how a real team resolved a bottleneck using the product's signature mechanism.
7. **The Cheatsheet / Reference Card:** A high-density visual one-sheet summarizing the entire methodology.
8. **The Framework Matrix:** A 2x2 diagram contrasting low-maturity vs. high-maturity practices.
9. **The Video Demonstration Script:** A 60-to-90 second shot-by-shot script engineered for short-form video (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn Video).
10. **The Technical Deep Dive:** A 1,500-word authoritative guide for technical publications or Substack.
11. **The Swipe File / Template Preview:** A transparent look inside the actual deliverable files.
12. **The Creator Collaboration One-Sheet:** A 1-page brief for partner creators outlining talking points, demonstration steps, and unique discount tracking links.

## 4. The Creator-Audience Content Funnel

For products distributed via creator partnerships, map the customer journey through these 12 stages:

```text
1. CREATOR CONTENT (Short-form hook or long-form teardown)
  → 2. ATTENTION (Pattern interrupt grounded in genuine pain)
  → 3. PROBLEM RECOGNITION ("That is exactly what happens on my team")
  → 4. CURIOSITY ("How did they solve that?")
  → 5. DEMONSTRATION (60-second screen capture of the signature mechanism)
  → 6. EMPIRICAL PROOF (Verified benchmarks, zero fabricated testimonials)
  → 7. CLEAR CALL TO ACTION ("Get the exact playbook & model below")
  → 8. CLEAN LANDING PAGE (Consistent typography, zero deceptive countdowns)
  → 9. FRICTIONLESS CHECKOUT (Explicit pricing, zero hidden subscriptions)
  → 10. INSTANT DELIVERY (< 60s delivery manifest with onboarding guide)
  → 11. TIME TO FIRST VALUE (< 180s to first successful action)
  → 12. ADVOCACY & REFERRAL (Customer shares reusable output with their team)
```

## 5. The Creator-Specific Presentation Layer

Keep the core product engine modular, robust, and reusable while engineering a tailored presentation layer for specific creator audiences:

$$\text{CORE PRODUCT ENGINE} + \text{CREATOR PRESENTATION LAYER}$$

### Configurable Presentation Elements
- **Creator Welcome Video / Message:** A dedicated greeting card on the first page or dashboard.
- **Domain-Specific Examples:** Tailoring pre-populated spreadsheet data to the creator's exact niche (e.g. B2B SaaS metrics vs. E-commerce D2C metrics).
- **Branded Accent Palette:** Adjusting secondary button colors or border tints to subtly align with creator aesthetics without compromising the core product identity.
- **Custom Bonus Worksheet:** A 1-page field exercise co-designed around the creator's signature philosophy.

*Ethical Invariant:* Never state or imply that a creator authored or endorsed the product unless a formal, verified commercial agreement exists.
