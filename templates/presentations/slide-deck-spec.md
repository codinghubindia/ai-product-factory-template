# AI PRODUCT FACTORY — PRESENTATION SLIDE DECK SPECIFICATION & STARTER

This specification defines the 16:9 widescreen layout system, typography scale, color discipline, and slide archetypes for generating professional presentation deliverables.

---

## 1. Canvas Dimensions & Geometry

- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen
- **Resolution:** 1920px x 1080px (or 13.33in x 7.5in)
- **Safe Margin:** 80px (0.83in) from all edges
- **Grid Layout:** 12-column horizontal grid with 24px gutters

---

## 2. Typographic Scale

- **Title / Action Header:** 32pt – 36pt Bold (Sans-serif, e.g. Inter / Arial / Helvetica Neue)
- **Subtitle / Category Tracker:** 14pt Semi-bold Uppercase with 0.08em letter spacing
- **Body Text:** 16pt Regular (Line-height 1.5)
- **Key Callout Metric:** 48pt – 64pt Extra Bold
- **Footnote / Citation:** 10pt Regular (Muted gray `#64748b`)

---

## 3. Core Slide Archetypes

### Slide 1: Executive Title / Hero
- **Top Label:** `EXECUTIVE BRIEFING &bull; DIGITAL PRODUCT SPECIFICATION`
- **Main Heading:** `Automating Compliance Audits for Multi-Cloud Environments`
- **Sub-heading:** `A High-Utility System for Slashing Audit Prep Time by 70%`
- **Footer Strip:** Author, Date, Version, Confidentiality.

### Slide 2: The Core Problem & Friction Matrix
- **Action Header:** `Manual Evidence Gathering Creates 140 Hours of Engineering Drag per Quarter`
- **Layout:** 3 balanced cards:
  1. *Friction 1 (Data Silos):* Manual screenshot capture across 12 consoles.
  2. *Friction 2 (Audit Fatigue):* Engineering leads spending 30% of time in meetings.
  3. *Friction 3 (Compliance Lag):* Audit findings arrive 60 days after drift occurs.

### Slide 3: Transformation Architecture
- **Action Header:** `Continuous Ingestion Replaces Periodic Fire Drills with Real-Time Compliance`
- **Layout:** Left-to-right 3-stage process flow (`Current State` &rarr; `Engine Process` &rarr; `Desired State`).

### Slide 4: Quantitative Impact / Metrics Grid
- **Action Header:** `Verified ROI Benchmarks from 40 SMB Deployments`
- **Layout:** 4 large metric blocks:
  - `72%` Reduction in audit preparation time
  - `$45K` Average annual savings in external consultant fees
  - `0` Missed compliance deadlines in 12 months
  - `< 15 min` Setup time via automated cloud connector

### Slide 5: Phased Implementation Roadmap
- **Action Header:** `Complete Deployment in Three Structured Sprints`
- **Layout:** 3-column timeline:
  - *Phase 1 (Days 1-7):* Credential connection & automated baseline scan.
  - *Phase 2 (Days 8-21):* Policy mapping & exception alerting setup.
  - *Phase 3 (Days 22-30):* Continuous reporting & stakeholder sign-off.

### Slide 6: Call-to-Action / Next Steps
- **Action Header:** `Initiate Pilot Deployment with Zero Production Disruption`
- **Layout:** Clear bulleted prerequisites, team access list, and link to documentation.
